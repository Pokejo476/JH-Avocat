Frequently asked questions

These answers provide general information: every situation is different, so do not hesitate to contact the firm for advice tailored to your case.

What should I do if I receive a notice of accounting audit or a summons to a tax audit?

Do not leave the letter unanswered, and do not wait for the first meeting with the auditor. These letters remind you that you may be assisted by an adviser of your choice: the earlier you involve one, the better the firm can prepare the exchanges, verify the regularity of the procedure and organize the documents to be provided. Contact the firm stating in the subject of your message that you have received a letter from the tax authorities, and mention any deadline indicated.

How quickly does the firm respond to my request?

The firm undertakes to reply as soon as possible. If your case is urgent, for example because a deadline to respond to the tax authorities is running, state this clearly in the subject of your message together with the deadline, so that it can be taken into account.

How does the first contact work, and how are fees set?

A complimentary 10-minute introductory phone call is offered. If you decide to proceed, services are generally billed at an hourly rate of €150 excl. VAT (+ applicable VAT). Depending on the nature and complexity of the case, a flat fee or an additional success fee may be agreed. Every engagement is covered by a written fee agreement.

Does the firm act outside Versailles?

Yes. The firm is based in Versailles and assists individuals and companies, French or foreign, throughout France. Meetings at the firm are by appointment only, and a first discussion can take place by phone.

Are my exchanges with the firm confidential?

Yes. Communications between a lawyer and their client are covered by professional secrecy. The information entered in the contact form is used only to process your request (see the legal notice). For a sensitive situation, avoid attaching detailed documents there: they can be sent afterwards according to the arrangements agreed with the firm.

Can I consult the firm about crypto-assets (trading, mining, NFTs)?

Yes. The firm assists with transactions involving cryptocurrencies, NFTs and digital assets: securing trading capital gains, mining, structuring investments and regularizing digital holdings.
