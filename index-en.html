PRACTICE AREAS

Corporate Taxation

Strategic advisory for company tax management: corporate income tax, taxable income calculation, tax consolidation, tax audits, real estate tax, corporate restructuring, and local business taxes.

VAT / Sales Tax

Domestic and cross-border transactions, taxability assessment, exemptions, applicable rates, real estate VAT, filing compliance, and VAT credit refund claims.

International Taxation

Cross-border tax planning, withholding taxes, inbound and outbound foreign investments, double tax treaty analysis.

Environmental Taxation

Excise duty regulations (TIC), general tax on polluting activities (TGAP), tax refunds, energy tax audits, and compliance.

Individual & Wealth Taxation

Personal income tax, real estate wealth tax (IFI), crypto-assets, estate planning, international mobility and expat support, and management of filing obligations.

Taxation of Crypto-assets and Digital Assets

Tax support for operations involving cryptocurrencies, NFTs, and digital assets: securing trading capital gains, mining, investment structuring, and digital asset regularization.

Tax Litigation & Audits

Comprehensive legal representation during tax audits (accounting audits, personal tax checks - ESFP), administrative claims, litigation before administrative courts, and tax rulings (rescrits fiscaux).

FEES

Fee transparency is a fundamental principle. From the initial consultation, fee structures and billing terms are clearly defined.

Initial Consultation

A complimentary 10-minute initial phone call is offered.

Hourly Rate

Services are generally billed on an hourly basis at €150 excl. VAT / hour (+ applicable VAT), based on the actual time spent on the case.

Flat Fee & Success Fee

Depending on the nature and complexity of the case, a flat fee arrangement or an agreement including a success fee may be agreed upon with the client.

Fee Agreement: In accordance with professional regulations, every instruction is formalized through a written fee agreement specifying billing terms, expenses, and costs.

Consumer Mediation: In the event of a dispute regarding legal fees, consumer clients may contact the Consumer Ombudsman for the Legal Profession: Sandra Werey, 180 bd Haussmann, 75008 Paris (https://mediateur-consommation-avocat.fr).
